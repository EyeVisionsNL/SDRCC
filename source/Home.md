# 📡 SDRCC — SDR Control Center

**Listen · Decode · Analyze · Share**

Welcome aboard! This wiki helps you install SDRCC, connect your receivers and get from a quiet dashboard to ships, aircraft, radio traffic and satellite reception.

## 🖥️ One station, several views

SDRCC runs on your Ubuntu computer. The desktop dashboard provides configuration, receiver management and mission planning. The optional Android companion connects to that same station for viewing, listening and selected controls.
