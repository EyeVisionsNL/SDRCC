## 🚢 Already receiving AIS?

[AIS-ATIS Bridge](https://eyevisionsnl.github.io/AIS-ATIS-Bridge/) is the lighter option for an existing AIS-catcher station with an extra RTL-SDR. It adds marine voice and ATIS/AIS matching without requiring aircraft or satellite features.
