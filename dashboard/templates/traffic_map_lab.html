Colours & information

AIS class colours: Cargo · Tanker · Passenger · High speed · Fishing · Class B. Orange outline: unverified. Faded: historical AIS position. Aircraft.

WTG: turquoise turbine symbol. Offshore platforms such as P15: amber platform symbol. Tugs keep their normal AIS-class symbol at low zoom and receive a raised wheelhouse and towing winch only in close-up 3D. Tankers show pipes and cargo ships show hatch covers. Deck details are illustrative.

Tracks: switch on to display received AIS-Catcher routes for all vessels currently on the map (up to the past hour). No estimated positions or fixed offshore-structure tracks; availability depends on AIS-Catcher's retained history.

White rings: KNRM; blue rings: Water police; green rings: fresh verified ATIS/AIS matches. These indicators never control the SDR or move the map.

Read-only positions from AIS-Catcher and readsb. VTS polygons come from the installed Rijnmond sector layer. Map Lab never starts or claims SDR receivers.
