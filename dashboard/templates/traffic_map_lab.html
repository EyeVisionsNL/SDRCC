Colours & information

AIS class colours: Cargo · Tanker · Passenger · High speed · Fishing · Class B. Orange outline: unverified. Faded: historical AIS position. Aircraft.

WTG: compact turquoise turbine symbol, sized similarly to platform markers. Fixed AIS aids such as Q13A-A PLATFORM have an amber platform marker even when PLATFORM is at the end of their name. Other offshore installations such as PLATFORM P15 C are recognized with stationary type-99 AIS evidence; a P-number alone is not enough. At close 3D zoom, ordinary Class B pleasure craft have a compact white/blue recreational boat model (with illustrative dimensions only if AIS lacks offsets); MSTX fast water taxis have a low black hull with yellow roof and stripes; KRVE HSC tenders have dark hulls, broad yellow fenders and a pale cabin with amber roof; Rotterdam Port Authority RPA vessels have blue hulls and yellow cabins/stripes; tugs have a wheelhouse and towing winch; police boats have blue/white cabins and a lightbar. All boats keep their ordinary AIS class icon when zoomed out. Tankers have pipes and cargo ships have hatch covers. Deck details are illustrative. Verified RPA 6/10/11/12/16 can use vessel-specific published dimensions (not AIS antenna offsets) if AIS measurements are missing; the popup marks approximations. Other RPA numbers retain the same 3D model when AIS hull dimensions are provided.

Tracks: switch on to display received AIS-Catcher routes for all vessels currently on the map. Adjust the history from 1 to 60 minutes (default 15); the setting is remembered. No estimated positions or fixed offshore-structure tracks; availability depends on AIS-Catcher's retained history.

ADS-B altitude colours (feet): ● <2,000 · ● 2–5k · ● 5–10k · ● 10–20k · ● 20–30k · ● 30k+ · ● unknown. Helicopters use a rotorcraft icon. AIS class-10 helicopters, like PILOT SERVICES SV, have their own orange helicopter symbol and are never drawn as ships. Credited ADS-B aircraft photos are requested on click only.

White rings: KNRM; blue rings: Water police; green rings: the last verified ATIS/AIS match (live for 30 seconds, then retained on this page). These indicators never control the SDR. Enable ATIS auto-center to move the camera once per new match; choose zoom 11–18. Manual map control remains available.

Read-only positions from AIS-Catcher and readsb. VTS polygons come from the installed Rijnmond sector layer. Map Lab never starts or claims SDR receivers.
