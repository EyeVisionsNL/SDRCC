THE SDRCC GUIDE / MARINE & ATIS

Which ship was that?

Follow the voice. Find the vessel. Replay the moment.

Listen to marine VHF

Open Traffic Voice → Marine Voice + AIS. Choose a fixed channel or scan list, then adjust Smart Gain or manual gain and the dB squelch.

The Marine channel filter and optional voice processing affect listening, not the separate ATIS decoding input. Excel channel-list import/export is available.

Which ship was that?

SDRCC validates the received ATIS identity and compares it with current AIS data. Possible Speaker shows a candidate vessel only when a suitable match exists; it is not a guaranteed identification.

Recordings, map & photos

  • Replay or save up to four recent Marine transmissions.
  • Desktop Auto can follow a matched vessel while keeping the map's zoom.
  • Android highlights the vessel but does not re-centre the map. Tap it for details.
  • Optional ship photos show the photographer and a link to the original.

The separate VTS Rijnmond overlay requires the map plugins. Enable VTS sector and SDRCC ATIS in the AIS map plugin selector, where installed.
